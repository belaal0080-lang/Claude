Clean-up and quality

Pause cutting
  • Every gap over 0.1 s removed, measured against each clip's own noise floor (the street noise means a fixed threshold misses every pause).
  • 40 ms handles kept so words aren't clipped.
  • Jump cuts hidden with alternating 100/112% scale.
Noise reduction
  • Wind and traffic rumble below 120 Hz removed (high-pass).
  • Spectral denoise learned from the gaps between your words.
  • De-ess, gentle compression, presence lift at 3–5 kHz, then −14 LUFS.
  • The gust at 18 s of IMG_9944 is softened.
Picture
  • Grade: warm the midtones, hold a cool sky so ember captions pop, lift shadows slightly. The raw is a little flat and blue.
  • Light stabilisation on the handheld moves.
  • Drone B-roll is 720p: upscaled to 1080 with Lanczos plus light sharpening and denoise.
  • Export H.264 High, 1080×1920, ~16 Mbps, so Instagram re-compresses it less.
