1. Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to read Founder Tribune, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control or screen magnification. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

2. What we do

  • Pages can be used with a keyboard alone, and the focused item is always visibly outlined.
  • A "Skip to content" link is the first item on every page.
  • Photographs carry a text description. Purely decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text meets contrast guidelines and can be enlarged to 200 percent without loss of content.
  • Pages adapt to phones and tablets and work in portrait or landscape.
  • Moving content, such as the funding ticker, pauses on hover or focus and stops entirely if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Forms have visible labels and say clearly what went wrong when something is missing.

3. Known limitations

  • Some older stories may have photo descriptions that are too brief.
  • Content from other providers, such as embedded video or social posts, may not meet the same standard.
  • Stories are loaded with JavaScript, so the site needs JavaScript turned on.

We have not yet had an independent accessibility audit. We will update this page when we do.

4. Tell us about a problem

If something on the site is hard or impossible for you to use, write to editor@foundertribune.com with the page address and what happened. We aim to reply within five working days and can send a story to you in another format.
