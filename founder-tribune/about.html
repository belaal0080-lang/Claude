About us

Founder Tribune reports on the people who start and run companies, and on how they actually do it.

Most business coverage follows the largest companies and the largest rounds. We write for the founder with eight employees and a hard decision to make this week.

We publish funding news, interviews, practical reporting on leadership and money, and opinion from people who have built something. We name our sources where we can and show the numbers behind a claim.

How we work

Our reporters decide what we cover. Nobody can pay for a news story, a headline or a favourable mention, and paid content is always labelled "Sponsored". We name our sources where we can, give the people we write about a fair chance to respond, and say plainly when we use AI tools. Read our full editorial standards.

Corrections

When we get something wrong, we fix it quickly and add a note to the story saying what changed. See our corrections policy or report an error.
