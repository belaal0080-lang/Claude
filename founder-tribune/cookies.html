1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, do the same job. They let a site remember you between pages and visits.

2. The types we use

4. How to change your choice

Select "Cookie settings" in the footer of any page to see the choices again. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work, but it will ask for your cookie choice again on each visit.
