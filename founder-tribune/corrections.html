Corrections

We correct our mistakes quickly and openly. Tell us if you see one.

Our corrections policy

  • Corrections. When a story contains a factual error, we fix the text and add a note at the end saying what was wrong, what it now says and the date.
  • Clarifications. When a story is accurate but could mislead, we reword it and add a note explaining the change.
  • Updates. When new information arrives after publication, we add it and mark the story as updated with the date.
  • Retractions. If a story is so flawed that it cannot be fixed, we withdraw it and publish an explanation in its place.

Minor fixes such as spelling do not get a note. We do not remove accurate stories because a subject would prefer them gone. Requests to remove personal information are considered by a senior editor case by case.

What to send us

Give us the link to the story, the sentence you believe is wrong, and what you believe is correct, with a source if you have one. An editor will check it and reply within three working days.
