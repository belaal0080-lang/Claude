Pitch your story

Tell us what you have built and what is new. Coverage is free and decided by our editors alone.

Three or four sentences: what happened, why now and what other founders can learn from it.
If your news is under embargo, tell us when it lifts.

What happens next

  1. An editor reads your pitchUsually within five working days.
  2. We reply if it fitsA reporter will ask for an interview and supporting numbers.
  3. We report and publishYou can check facts and quotes. You cannot approve the story.

What makes a strong pitch

  • Something new: a round, a launch, a result
  • Real numbers you are willing to share
  • A lesson another founder could use
  • A founder available to talk on the record
