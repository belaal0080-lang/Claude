Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Founder Tribune collects when you use this website, why we collect it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect the information you give us, such as your email address when you subscribe to a newsletter, and the details you enter in our contact, pitch and advertising forms. We also collect basic usage data, such as pages visited and the type of device used.

How we use it

We use your information to send newsletters you asked for, reply to your messages, understand which stories readers find useful and keep the site secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use cookies and local storage to remember your preferences and to measure readership. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work without them.

Sharing

We share information with service providers that help us run the site, such as email delivery and analytics companies, and when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any newsletter using the link in each email. You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to editor@foundertribune.com.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
