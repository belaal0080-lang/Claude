1. Who we are

Founder Tribune is published by [Company legal name], [Registered postal address] ("Founder Tribune", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

This policy explains what we collect when you read our website, subscribe to a newsletter, contact us or send us a story pitch, and what choices you have. Questions go to privacy@foundertribune.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Newsletter sign-ups: your email address and the newsletters you choose.
  • Contact, correction and advertising forms: your name, email address, company and the content of your message.
  • Story pitches: your name, email, role, company, website and what you tell us about your story.
  • Tips and documents you send to our journalists.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: pages viewed, the time of your visit, the page that referred you, browser type, device type, screen size and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Cookies and local storage: small files that remember your choices. See our cookie policy.
  • Server logs: IP address and request details, kept for security and fault-finding.

Information from other sources

Our journalists gather information about people and companies from interviews, public records and other published sources as part of their reporting. Email and analytics providers may tell us whether a newsletter was delivered or opened.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies and local storage to run the site and remember your cookie choice. With your permission we may also use analytics and advertising cookies. You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer of every page. Details are in our cookie policy.

5. Newsletters and marketing

We send newsletters only to people who sign up. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to us to be removed. We may measure opens and clicks to learn which stories are useful. We do not sell or rent our subscriber list.

6. Who we share information with

  • Service providers that work for us under contract, such as web hosting, email delivery, analytics, form handling and security services. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Advertising and sponsorship partners, where you have agreed to advertising cookies.
  • Artificial intelligence providers: when we use AI tools to help research or draft a story, the text we send them may include information already gathered for that story. We do not send them reader account or newsletter data.
  • Authorities and legal advisers, when the law requires it or to protect our rights, our staff or the public.
  • A successor, if the publication is sold or merged. We will tell subscribers before their information is used under a different policy.

We do not sell personal information for money.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where the law requires it, we use approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for those transfers. You can ask us for details.

8. How long we keep information

  • Newsletter details: until you unsubscribe, then a suppression record so we do not email you again.
  • Messages, pitches and correction requests: up to three years after our last contact.
  • Server and security logs: up to twelve months.
  • Analytics data: up to twenty-six months, in aggregated form where possible.
  • Published journalism and our reporting records are kept as an archive.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and regular backups to protect information. No online service can promise complete security. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

10. Your rights and choices

Everyone

  • Unsubscribe from any newsletter using the link in the email.
  • Change your cookie choice using "Cookie settings" in the footer.
  • Ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

You have the right to access, correct, delete and receive a copy of your information, to restrict or object to our use of it, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may complain to your data protection authority. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

California and other US states

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to delete it; to correct it; and to opt out of its sale or of sharing for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Where advertising cookies count as "sharing", you can opt out by choosing "Essential only" in Cookie settings. We honour Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out where the law requires. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

How to make a request

Write to privacy@foundertribune.com or to [Company legal name], [Registered postal address]. We may need to confirm your identity. We reply within one month, or within 45 days where US state law applies. An authorised agent may make a request for you with written permission.

11. Journalism and confidential sources

Privacy laws contain exemptions for journalism so that the press can report in the public interest. When we hold information for reporting, some of the rights above may be limited where using them would undermine that reporting or reveal a confidential source. We protect the identity of confidential sources. If you want to send us sensitive material, read the guidance on our contact page first.

12. Children

Founder Tribune is written for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, tell us and we will delete it.

13. Links to other sites

Our stories link to other websites, and our share buttons take you to other services. Their privacy practices are their own, so read their policies.

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will tell newsletter subscribers by email before it takes effect.
