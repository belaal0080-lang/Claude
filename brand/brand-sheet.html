Ember

A fast, talk-to-camera property and lifestyle reel style. Big keyword captions, one hot colour, punch-in zooms on the words that sell, and a short on-beat montage before the proof. It's built from what the three reference reels share, with its own type, colour and motion signature.

Caption rules

  • One keyword per beat, 1–2 words, caps. If it doesn't sell, it's a subtitle.
  • Modifier sits under the keyword, indented 28% from its left edge.
  • Subtitles run word by word under the stack. The spoken word is full bone with an ember underline, the rest at 45%.
  • Roman-Urdu spelled one consistent way per video ("hai", not "hy"/"hai" mixed).
  • Numbers (prices, %, dates) always go on a number card, never in a subtitle.

Placement at 1080×1920

  • Safe zone: 220 px top, 420 px bottom, 120 px right, 64 px left.
  • Stack starts at y = 1000 px, left-aligned at x = 86 px.
  • Monogram: 72 px square, 60 px from top-right safe edge.
  • Soft ink shadow: 0 4 24 px at 35%. No outlines, no boxes.

Hook: bold claim in the first 3 s, caption on frame 1 Burst: 8–10 B-roll cuts at 0.5 s, on the beat Proof: one 5–6 s hold (drone reveal or render) CTA: end card

Cuts

  • Target median shot 1.6 s, never over 3 s except the proof hold.
  • Cut on the consonant, not the pause. Remove every breath.
  • Jump-cut the host freely. Change scale on each jump (100 → 112%).

Zooms

  • Punch-in: hard 112% cut on keywords, 2 per 10 s at most.
  • Drift: slow 100 → 106% push over any B-roll shot longer than 2 s.
  • No wobble or shake presets.

Transitions

  • Default is a hard cut.
  • Section change: 3-frame ember flash (ember at 60%, screen blend).
  • Into the burst: 8-frame whip pan with motion blur.
06 · Motion graphics, music and sound

Motion and audio spec

Motion graphics

  • Ember swipe (signature): bar scales 0 → 100% left to right in 4 frames, ease-out. Keyword lands from 114% with a 3 px blur over 3 frames. The modifier rises 10 px a frame later.
  • Map pin: satellite still, ember outline drawn on over 12 frames, pin drops with a 2-frame overshoot.
  • One hero 3D moment per video: the project name tracked onto a real surface (façade, hoarding, road).
  • Exit: everything cuts out with the shot. No fade-outs on captions.

Music and sound

  • Master: −14 LUFS integrated, true peak −1 dBTP, loudness range 5–7 LU (both references sit at −14.1 and −14.2).
  • Voice: up front. Music ducked 15–18 dB under speech, full level only during the burst.
  • Music: 95–110 BPM, modern percussive, a clear drop where the burst starts. Licensed tracks only.
  • SFX: soft tick on every ember swipe, whoosh on whips, short riser into the proof hold, sub hit on the number card.
  • Out: music tail rings 0.8 s past the last word, then cut.

Do

  • One accent, one keyword at a time.
  • Shoot golden hour, host in a dark solid outfit.
  • Show the place: drone, walk-in, render, real site.
  • Pin the phone number on the end card.

Don't

  • Stock rainbow light leaks or glow text.
  • Script fonts, or two accent colours on screen at once.
  • Captions in the bottom 420 px (Reels UI covers them).
  • Music louder than voice outside the burst.
